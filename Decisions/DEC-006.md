# DEC-006 — Admit Device-Qualified Native Texture Arrays Conditionally into Production

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Scope:** WO-056 Phase K; production engine/GL4ES/Diffusion texture-array capability admission
- **Finding:** The next bounded experiment is to admit the device-qualified native texture-array capability into production only through a complete, conditional capability contract. Engine and Diffusion must agree on support, required GL4ES/native ES3 entry points and limits must be present, and the real terrain shader/material route must remain intact. An extension-string-only change is not sufficient.

## Why it matters

WO-056 Phase J proved the Apple GLES3/GL4ES array object, upload, shader translation/reflection, sampler, draw, readback, cleanup, and lifecycle contract on device. Production still reports `GL_EXT_texture_array - failed`, so Diffusion never enables `R_TEXTURE_ARRAY_EXT`, `GLSL_ALLOW_TEXTURE_ARRAY`, or the real landscape array route. Phase K converts the qualified primitive into a guarded production capability without weakening the existing terrain feature set or bypassing the renderer architecture.

## Evidence

- Bundle 116 / candidate `bc4b2b7181b3111053f14ff86e8ff634718acf30`, workflow `32489923843`, passed WO-056 Phase J on iPhone 16 Pro Max / iOS 26.6.
- Engine `GL_CheckExtension("GL_EXT_texture_array", ..., "gl_texture_2d_array", GL_TEXTURE_ARRAY_EXT, 0)` gates `GL_TEXTURE_ARRAY_EXT` and `GL_MAX_ARRAY_TEXTURE_LAYERS_EXT`.
- Diffusion independently gates `R_TEXTURE_ARRAY_EXT`; that state controls `GLSL_ALLOW_TEXTURE_ARRAY`, `LOAD_TEXTURE_ARRAY`, and landscape diffuse/normal array use.
- The iOS Diffusion shader patch currently filters `MULTI_LAYERS`, so Phase K must audit and preserve the real terrain shader contract before advertising support.
- [WO-056](../WorkOrders/WO-056.md) and [evidence manifest](../Evidence/WO-056/manifest.md).

## What this does not establish

This decision does not itself prove that production terrain compiles, loads, renders correctly, or passes on device. It does not qualify ordinary gameplay or the quarantined `ch1map1` transition. Those require later evidence after a coherent Phase K candidate is build-qualified and reviewed.

## Superseded decisions

None. This advances beyond DEC-005 while preserving DEC-001's rule that native conformance must precede terrain admission.
