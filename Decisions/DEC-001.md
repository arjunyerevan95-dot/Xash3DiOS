# DEC-001 — Complete Native Texture-Array Conformance Before Terrain Admission

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Scope:** Diffusion renderer admission on iOS
- **Finding:** Native `GL_TEXTURE_2D_ARRAY` support must satisfy the complete conformance contract before the terrain path is enabled or advertised. Build/static qualification alone is insufficient; the final boundary remains physical-device evidence from the normal Diffusion bootstrap route.

## Why it matters

Diffusion terrain depends on texture-array behavior across symbol exposure, storage, upload, sampling, draw, readback, and presentation. Treating a partial signal as terrain readiness would conceal renderer faults and weaken the project’s established gate discipline.

## Evidence

- Work Orders 50 and 53–56 in the authoritative project ledger establish the staged array-conformance sequence.
- WO-056 Phase H Bundle 114 supplied normal-bootstrap device evidence up to the uniform-write failure boundary.
- WO-056 Phase I candidate `bc4b2b7181b3111053f14ff86e8ff634718acf30` and Actions run `32489923843` satisfy the build/static contract for the corrected implementation.
- [WO-056 evidence manifest](../Evidence/WO-056/manifest.md)

## What this does not establish

This decision does not device-accept Bundle 116, qualify the Diffusion terrain renderer, or qualify `ch1map1`. Those outcomes require separately authorized and recorded evidence.

## Superseded decisions

This supersedes proposals to admit terrain using only extension exposure, partial array operations, or fallback/emulation evidence.
