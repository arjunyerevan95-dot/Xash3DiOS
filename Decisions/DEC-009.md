# DEC-009 - Preserve Phase N Outcome B and Repair the First Provider-Admission Divergence

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Scope:** WO-056 Phase N device evidence and Phase O ordinary-runtime GL4ES provider admission
- **Finding:** Accept the single Bundle 126 device run as Phase N Outcome B. Preserve its partial progress through ordinary Diffusion map load and drawable presentation, but reject production texture-array admission and visible-scene qualification. Investigate and, only when source-proven, correct the first deterministic provider/engine divergence before addressing the later shader-family failures or unresolved termination.

## Why it matters

Bundle 116 already proves that native ES3 texture-array object, upload, sampler, shader, draw, readback, cleanup, and lifecycle primitives work on this device. Bundle 126 proves the exact ordinary argument route and reaches `ch1map0`, but the engine receives four callbacks while `GL_EXT_texture_array` is absent and `GL_MAX_ARRAY_TEXTURE_LAYERS` resolves to zero. Diffusion consequently disables landscapes before the later Bmodel/Studio shader failures occur. Keeping Phase O on that earliest deterministic disagreement preserves the observation -> hypothesis -> bounded experiment sequence and prevents the crash, shader-LOD, model-link, and texture-array problems from being merged into one unqualified patch.

## What this does not establish

This decision does not establish that the absent provider marker means `GetHardwareExtensions` never ran; logging transport and execution must be distinguished. It does not prove the cause of the zero layer limit, the later shader failures, or the final process termination. It does not authorize unconditional extension advertisement, a fabricated capability limit, a direct native bypass outside GL4ES, terrain fallback, shader-LOD/model changes, gameplay qualification, `ch1map1`, or a Phase O device test.

## Phase O worker outcome

Outcome A is worker-reported and build-qualified at Bundle 130. The complete source audit proved that the current-context/provider lifecycle itself was correctly ordered, but the stock generic ESSL test concatenated `#version 300 es` directly with an invalid extension directive. That deterministic compiler failure kept `hardext.glsl300es` false and caused the already-conditional `BuildExtensionsList` predicate to omit `GL_EXT_texture_array`.

The correction remains at the GL4ES provider owner: it uses one complete, stage-correct ESSL 300 vertex source, retains the live ES3/procedure/layer/route predicates, and exposes only a read-only cached provider snapshot for an engine-log marker. No token, limit, native path, terrain consumer, or device result is fabricated. Candidate `f42f2c96b61624fe510fe32288bfbfa6873cc686`, workflow `32570119378`, job `97024299913`, and artifact `9475150885` passed complete arm64/IPA qualification. Physical-device admission remains unaccepted pending a later explicit orchestrator decision.
