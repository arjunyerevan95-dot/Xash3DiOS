## Authority and objective

Adopt the complete Phase H evidence. Determine the exact source-proven origin of `GL_INVALID_OPERATION` in the native texture-array sampling/readback stage and correct the complete failing contract in one coherent change. Preserve every device-proven PASS stage and the revised normal-Diffusion bootstrap. This is not authorization for a succession of one-call patches or a separate instrumentation-only IPA.

The goal is a build-qualified candidate whose existing harness still creates and uploads distinct array layers, translates and reflects `sampler2DArray`, draws four quadrants from layers 0, 1, 2, and 3 through the real native ES3/GL4ES route, reads back the rendered pixels, verifies the source-derived expected checksum, exercises deletion/recreation and lifecycle ownership, emits a bounded terminal result, and exits before Diffusion module or gameplay startup.

## Required audit before implementation

1. Trace every call and state transition in the sampling/readback stage from the last successful shader/reflection operation through framebuffer and attachment setup, viewport/scissor, program and uniform binding, texture-unit and `GL_TEXTURE_2D_ARRAY` binding, vertex/index/attribute state, draw submission, synchronization, read-buffer selection, pixel-pack state, `glReadPixels`, checksum calculation, state restoration, and lifecycle continuation.
2. Build a call-level error-provenance table. For every potentially failing call, record the exact wrapper/native entry point, active target and object identities, framebuffer and drawable ownership, legal GLES version and arguments, required preconditions, current state provider, and whether GL4ES forwards, translates, emulates, or caches the operation. Prove that the reported `0x0502` belongs to this stage and is not stale from an earlier successful stage.
3. Audit framebuffer completeness and format compatibility, draw/read framebuffer binding, draw-buffer/read-buffer legality, attachment dimensions and sample counts, default-versus-offscreen framebuffer semantics, viewport dimensions, shader link/use state, sampler uniform type and unit, layer-coordinate generation, vertex-array and attribute validity, pixel format/type, pack alignment, destination-buffer size, and any required finish/fence ordering on Apple GLES3.
4. Trace the checksum contract to its source data. Record the exact per-layer colors or patterns, quadrant-to-layer mapping, drawable or offscreen dimensions, coordinate origin, channel order, row order, and expected checksum derivation. The correction must not merely bless checksum `a915906d`, weaken comparison, or accept uninitialized/unchanged pixels.
5. Prove whether the defect is in the harness's GLES3 call sequence, the GL4ES wrapper's state/forwarding route, the direct-drawable integration, or an interaction among them. Audit all relevant cleanup and state restoration so the fix cannot make lifecycle PASS by leaking objects or skipping the tested operation.

## Completed Outcome A

- Exact logged origin: `gl4es_glUniform1i(u_Array, 0)`.
- Structural cause: `GL_SAMPLER_2D_ARRAY` was missing from GL4ES `uniformsize`, `is_uniform_int`, and `n_uniform`, causing incompatible uniform size/class handling and deterministic `GL_INVALID_OPERATION`.
- Additional complete-route defect: the old harness used a CPU client pointer with `glVertexAttribPointer`, invalid in native OpenGL ES 3; Bundle 116 uses a VBO-backed quad.
- Checksum contract: FNV-1a-32 over red, green, magenta, and yellow RGBA quadrant bytes equals `0xA915906D`, exactly matching Bundle 114's successful readback pixels.
- Machine-readable contract: `scripts/ios/wo56i-sampling-readback-contract.json`.
- Candidate: `bc4b2b7181b3111053f14ff86e8ff634718acf30`.
- Qualifying workflow: `32489923843`, job `96794910555`, success.
- Artifact: `Xash3DiOS-arm64-unsigned`, ID `9449473335`.
